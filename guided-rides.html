The members of Airborne have arranged to give guided rides at either the Ft. Pierce bike trail or Halpatiokee. If you want to learn the trail from someone who knows the directions, the best line, what to avoid, then contact us and we'll try to arrange a time to introduce you to the trail.

The Facebook is the place to go to arrange a guided trail ride. Simply start a new topic for "Guided Ride?" in the section for whichever trail. One of our members will post a reply. You don't have to be a dues paying member to sign up for the forum. We do request you sign up - it's to cut out spammers.

We are always looking for conscientious riders to lead individuals and groups through either park. If you would like to help us and become a ride guide contact club president John Doe at john-doe@aol.com
